About

Since the 13th century, the Tschopp family story has been one of courage, service, and steadfast devotion to community, beginning in the historic town of Чоп, nestled in the fertile lowlands of Transcarpathia in modern day Ukraine near the borders of Hungary and Slovakia. In these early years, our ancestors stood as defenders of Western Europe's eastern frontiers, protecting their neighbors and common folk from threats and turmoil, earning a reputation for bravery and fairness. In the 14th century, driven by resilience and hope, the some of the family journeyed westward, seeking new opportunities among the valleys and mountains of Switzerland. There, surrounded by Alpine tranquility, our lineage flourished, blending Eastern determination with Swiss craftsmanship and tradition. Today, our family tree proudly stretches across continents, with descendants establishing vibrant communities and meaningful lives around the globe—united by shared history, enduring values, and an ongoing commitment to innovation, service, and the preservation of our rich heritage for generations yet to come.
